Common questions

Questions, answered.

Is there a minimum to work with you?

No. Investment management is available at any asset level for a flat 1.00% annual advisory fee. Financial planning is available separately or included with management at $300,000 or more in managed assets.

Can I get financial planning without investment management?

Yes. Choose a one-time engagement starting at $2,500 or a planning membership starting at $300 per month.

Are there hidden fees?

The advisory fee is separate from third-party charges such as custodian fees or internal ETF expenses. These are disclosed by the custodian and fund.

How do I reach my advisor?

By call, text, or email. Start with a free 30-minute conversation, with no obligation.
