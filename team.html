Founder & CEO

Steven Kibbel

A planning-first perspective for professionals and business owners who want a clearer view of their financial lives.

Steven founded Kibbel Financial Planning in 2020 after more than a decade in financial services. His work brings retirement planning, investments, tax considerations, cash flow, and long-term goals into one practical conversation.

Raised in Nashville, Steven is a husband and father of four. Away from the office, he enjoys hiking and backpacking with his family, and writes children's books to encourage early financial literacy.

Director of Operations

Teresa Kibbel

Organized, attentive support that helps clients feel informed and cared for throughout the planning process.

A Nashville native, Teresa brings more than eight years of experience in financial services and client experience. She coordinates communications and details with care, professionalism, and discretion.

Working alongside her brother, Teresa helps keep each client's experience clear and well organized, so people know what to expect and where to turn with questions.
